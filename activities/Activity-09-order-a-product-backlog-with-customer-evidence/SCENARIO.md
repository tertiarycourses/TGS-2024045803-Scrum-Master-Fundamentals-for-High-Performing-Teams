## Scenario

A food-delivery platform has forty candidate items from customers, operations, fraud, accessibility, and growth teams. Every stakeholder labels their request high priority, and hidden dependencies cause rework during Sprints.

## Questions to answer

1. Which evidence supports value?
2. What risk or learning should move earlier?
3. Which dependency changes the order?
4. Where must Product Owner judgement override a score?

## Scenario constraints

- Keep the Product Goal, Sprint Goal, and Definition of Done visible where relevant.
- Do not transfer Product Owner or Developer accountability to the Scrum Master.
- Separate facts, observations, interpretations, and decisions.
- Use evidence to choose an adaptation; do not rank individuals.
