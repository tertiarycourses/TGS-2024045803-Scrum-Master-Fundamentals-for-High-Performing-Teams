# Activity 03 - Coach a Team Through an Impediment

**Course:** Scrum Master Fundamentals for High-Performing Teams

## Scenario

A retail platform team loses two days each Sprint waiting for a security review. Developers want the Scrum Master to chase approvals; Security says requests arrive incomplete; the Product Owner keeps adding urgent compliance items mid-Sprint.

## Questions to answer

1. What makes this an impediment rather than ordinary work?
2. Who is closest to each decision?
3. What systemic condition sustains the delay?
4. How will the team know the experiment worked?

## Scenario constraints

- Keep the Product Goal, Sprint Goal, and Definition of Done visible where relevant.
- Do not transfer Product Owner or Developer accountability to the Scrum Master.
- Separate facts, observations, interpretations, and decisions.
- Use evidence to choose an adaptation; do not rank individuals.
