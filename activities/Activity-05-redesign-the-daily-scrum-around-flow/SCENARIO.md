## Scenario

The Daily Scrum at BrightBank lasts 35 minutes. Each Developer reports to the Scrum Master while others multitask. Blockers repeat, work piles up in testing, and the Sprint Goal is not mentioned.

## Questions to answer

1. What decision must the Daily Scrum enable?
2. Which items threaten the Sprint Goal?
3. What should happen after the 15 minutes?
4. How can the Scrum Master coach without taking over?

## Scenario constraints

- Keep the Product Goal, Sprint Goal, and Definition of Done visible where relevant.
- Do not transfer Product Owner or Developer accountability to the Scrum Master.
- Separate facts, observations, interpretations, and decisions.
- Use evidence to choose an adaptation; do not rank individuals.
