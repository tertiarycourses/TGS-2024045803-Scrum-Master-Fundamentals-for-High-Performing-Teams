## Acceptance checklist

- [ ] Covers Product Owner, Scrum Master, and Developers
- [ ] Separates accountability from job title
- [ ] Aligns with organisational policy
- [ ] Makes decision and escalation rules observable

## Scrum quality gate

- [ ] The relevant Product Goal or Sprint Goal is stated.
- [ ] Scrum accountabilities remain clear.
- [ ] Evidence is separated from assumptions.
- [ ] The decision and accountable owner are recorded.
- [ ] A review trigger or measure is defined.
- [ ] The completed workbook opens without formula errors.
- [ ] No confidential or personal data has been added.
