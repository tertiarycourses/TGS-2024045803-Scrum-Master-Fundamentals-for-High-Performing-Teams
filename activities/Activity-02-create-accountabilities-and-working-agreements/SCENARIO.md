## Scenario

A digital payments team has a Product Owner, Scrum Master, developers, architects, and line managers, but decisions are slow. Managers assign Sprint tasks, the Scrum Master writes status reports, and the Product Owner shares backlog control with a steering committee.

## Questions to answer

1. Which accountability is currently diluted?
2. What can be delegated without transferring accountability?
3. How will policies be respected without removing self-management?
4. Which norm makes disagreement safe and useful?

## Scenario constraints

- Keep the Product Goal, Sprint Goal, and Definition of Done visible where relevant.
- Do not transfer Product Owner or Developer accountability to the Scrum Master.
- Separate facts, observations, interpretations, and decisions.
- Use evidence to choose an adaptation; do not rank individuals.
