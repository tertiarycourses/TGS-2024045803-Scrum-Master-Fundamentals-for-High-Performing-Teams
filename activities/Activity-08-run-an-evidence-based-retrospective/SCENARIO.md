## Scenario

A media team has repeated the same Retrospective actions for three Sprints. Participation is uneven, actions have no owners, and the team blames an external dependency without testing alternatives.

## Questions to answer

1. Which data contradicts the team's story?
2. What is within influence?
3. Which experiment is small enough to learn quickly?
4. How will the action enter the next Sprint?

## Scenario constraints

- Keep the Product Goal, Sprint Goal, and Definition of Done visible where relevant.
- Do not transfer Product Owner or Developer accountability to the Scrum Master.
- Separate facts, observations, interpretations, and decisions.
- Use evidence to choose an adaptation; do not rank individuals.
