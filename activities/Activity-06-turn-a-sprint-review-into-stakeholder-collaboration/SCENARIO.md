## Scenario

An e-commerce team demos completed screens at Sprint Review, collects compliments, and ends. Customer-service data shows a rise in checkout confusion, but stakeholders see the backlog only after priorities are finalized.

## Questions to answer

1. What changed in the operating environment?
2. Which evidence should influence ordering?
3. How is a Review different from a demo?
4. What experiment reduces the largest uncertainty?

## Scenario constraints

- Keep the Product Goal, Sprint Goal, and Definition of Done visible where relevant.
- Do not transfer Product Owner or Developer accountability to the Scrum Master.
- Separate facts, observations, interpretations, and decisions.
- Use evidence to choose an adaptation; do not rank individuals.
