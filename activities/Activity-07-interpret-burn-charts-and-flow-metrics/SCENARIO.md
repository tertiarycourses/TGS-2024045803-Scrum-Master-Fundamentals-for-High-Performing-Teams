## Scenario

A public-services team reports stable velocity, but work regularly carries over and stakeholders are surprised by scope changes. Leaders want a dashboard to compare teams, while Developers want evidence that helps them improve.

## Questions to answer

1. What does each chart reveal and conceal?
2. Which change is scope rather than delivery?
3. What additional evidence is needed before acting?
4. Which metric supports the Sprint Goal decision?

## Scenario constraints

- Keep the Product Goal, Sprint Goal, and Definition of Done visible where relevant.
- Do not transfer Product Owner or Developer accountability to the Scrum Master.
- Separate facts, observations, interpretations, and decisions.
- Use evidence to choose an adaptation; do not rank individuals.
