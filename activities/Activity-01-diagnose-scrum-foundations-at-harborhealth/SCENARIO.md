## Scenario

HarborHealth is replacing a patient-booking service. Leaders approved a twelve-month requirements plan, but regulations, clinic workflows, and patient expectations are changing monthly. Teams report defects late and customer feedback arrives only at final acceptance.

## Questions to answer

1. Where does uncertainty invalidate the existing plan?
2. Which feedback loop should be shortened first?
3. How do transparency, inspection, and adaptation operate in the proposal?
4. What waste or defect signal should improve?

## Scenario constraints

- Keep the Product Goal, Sprint Goal, and Definition of Done visible where relevant.
- Do not transfer Product Owner or Developer accountability to the Scrum Master.
- Separate facts, observations, interpretations, and decisions.
- Use evidence to choose an adaptation; do not rank individuals.
